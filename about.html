Founded in 2015

How It Started

In 2015, CEO Faisal Aslam recognized a significant gap in Pakistan's luxury automotive market: buyers lacked verified vehicle histories and genuine mileage guarantees. Elite Motors was established to set a new standard of trust, connecting Lahore's automotive enthusiasts directly with certified zero-meter imports from Europe and Japan.

  • ✓ Direct zero-meter factory sourcing with transparent customs verification.
  • ✓ 100% digital history reports and genuine auction grading sheets.
Complete Transparency

Our Philosophy

We believe that acquiring a flagship vehicle should be a seamless, empowering journey. We eliminate non-genuine claims and hidden fees by providing full computerized diagnostic disclosures, battery health audits for electric vehicles, and complete price clarity upfront.
